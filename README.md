# Vice Pack (Minecraft Bedrock add-on)

Beer, wine, liquor, moonshine, shots, coffee, tea, kava, White Monster, cigarettes, cigars, a pipe, a vape, a hookah,
Zynn, joints, bongs, weed, cocaine, crack, meth, Adderall, ecstasy, LSD, shrooms, ketamine, Xanax, lean, opium,
morphine and heroin. Each one has its own effects, colour haze, sounds and particles, and each one can be put down in
the world. Grow your own weed, tobacco, opium poppies, coffee and tea; brew, distill, cure, grind and cook in your own
bar and lab. Habits form, withdrawal hurts, bad combinations and overdoses can kill you. It has nothing to do with any
other add-on.

**Install:** open `Vice_Pack_v2_0.mcaddon`, then turn on both packs (Behavior + Resource) for the world.
Needs Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.90 or newer. Everything is in one collapsible **VICES** group in the creative inventory
(Items tab). Craft a **Vice Guide** (book + sugar) for the in-game guide, your status and the settings.

## Using things

- **Hold use** on a drink, smoke, pill, pouch, syringe or tab to use it.
- **Put anything down:** sneak + use on the top of a block. Then **use the placed one** to have it from there.
- **Cocaine and ketamine** are cut into **lines** on whatever you use them on (up to 3). Use them to snort one: the
  camera leans down to the table and sweeps along the line.
- **Reusables:** the bong burns **weed**, the pipe **cured tobacco**, the vape **vape juice**; they stay.
- **Friends:** sneak + use while looking at a player **passes** them what you're holding. Sneak + use a drink in the
  air **buys a round** for everyone near you. Standing in someone's weed smoke gives you a **contact high**.

## Growing

Use the seed on grass, dirt or farmland; crops grow in 4 stages (bone meal speeds them up); use a grown one to harvest.
Breaking grass sometimes drops a seed.

| Plant with | Harvest |
|---|---|
| **Weed** | 2–3 weed |
| **Tobacco Seeds** | 2–4 tobacco leaves (+ maybe seeds) |
| **Opium Poppy Seeds** | 1–2 opium (+ seeds) |
| **Coffee Beans** | 2–4 coffee beans |
| **Tea Leaves** | 2–3 tea leaves |

## Stations (crafting by doing)

| Station | Recipe | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Brew Barrel** | barrel + 2 iron ingots | Add up to 8 **wheat** (beer) or **sweet berries** (wine). About 3 minutes (keeps going even when you're away), then fill **glass bottles** from it. Foam on top = ready |
| **Still** | 3 copper ingots + bucket | Add up to 8 **potatoes** (liquor) or **sugar cane** (moonshine). Needs a **fire under it** (campfire, fire, lava, magma, lit furnace); 90 s of heat. Fill glass bottles |
| **Drying Rack** | 4 sticks + string | Hang up to 4 **tobacco leaves**; about 2 minutes later they're **cured tobacco** (cigarettes, cigars, Zynn, pipe, hookah, vape juice) |
| **Lab Table** | crafting table + brewing stand | Cook **cocaine → crack**, **opium → heroin**, **redstone + sugar → meth**, **quartz + sugar → ketamine**. 30 s. One batch in ten goes wrong: a fire flash or toxic fumes |
| **Coffee Grinder** | iron ingot + oak planks + stick | **Coffee beans → ground coffee** |
| **Kettle** | 2 iron ingots + iron nugget | On a fire, filled with a **water bucket** (4 cups): use a **bowl** with ground coffee or tea leaves on you → coffee / tea, with a whistle |
| **Coffee Machine** | 2 iron ingots + redstone + glass pane | A **bowl** with ground coffee on you → coffee. No fire, no water |

## Bad combinations

You get a red **⚠** warning when you mix:
- **Downers** (alcohol, opioids, Xanax): your breathing gets shallow, darkness, and it can tip into a **deadly
  overdose**; opioids + Xanax almost always do.
- **Coke + booze:** heart strain, sometimes an overdose.
- **Coffee, Monster or speed + booze:** you feel fine (the drunk effects and the bar hide) but you're just as drunk.
- **Ecstasy + booze:** overheating. **Ecstasy + coke or speed:** heart overdose.
- **Coke + speed:** your chest goes tight.
- **Weed + booze:** crossfaded, you'll probably throw up. **Weed + a trip:** the trip gets longer.

## How it feels

- **Overdoses take 30 seconds:** blackouts, a racing (or fading) heartbeat, Wither, then your heart gives out /
  your breathing stops (**deadly**). **Narcaine** saves you.
- **Habits** (nicotine, alcohol, caffeine, opioids, stimulants, benzos) form with use and stay with you. Go without
  and you get **withdrawal** every 25–50 s until you use again or it fades.
- **Nodding off** (heroin, opium, lean), **paranoia** (meth, crack, cocaine, bad trips), **out of body** (ketamine),
  **vomiting**, **double vision** and **hangovers** (drinking), **crashes** after uppers.
- **Sleep:** you **can't sleep while wired**. A night's sleep clears hangovers, crashes and comedowns and eases every
  habit a little. Coming down from meth or crack at night draws **phantoms**.
- **Recovery:** your clean streak counts from your last habit-forming use. **1 day, 3 days, 1 week and 30 days clean**
  each earn a Recovery Chip; using again after a day clean is a relapse.
- **Drunk talk:** your chat comes out slurred. *This only works if the game lets add-on scripts read chat (it's an
  experimental feature in some versions); otherwise chat is left alone.*
- **Sounds:** the lighter, can, pour, cough, sniff, bubbles, exhale, pills, kettle whistle, grinder and pool balls are
  the pack's own (made by `tools/vice_sounds.py`).
- **Colour haze** for every high, easing in and out; only blackouts go fully black.

## Settings (Vice Guide → Settings)

Haze strength (0–100%), camera effects (shakes, nodding, out-of-body, snort camera, blackouts), overdoses,
habits and withdrawal, hallucinations, slurred chat. They're saved with the world and apply to everyone.
`/scriptevent vice:menu` opens the same menu; `/scriptevent vice:sober` clears you.
